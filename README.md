# Danny-s-Diner
A SQL analysis of a small restaurant's customer data, answering 10 business questions about visiting patterns, spending, favourite menu items and a loyalty program. This is Case Study #1 of Danny Ma's 8 Week SQL Challenge, solved in MySQL 8.0.

Business problem

Danny opened a small restaurant selling three dishes: sushi, curry and ramen. He has captured a few months of basic data (sales, menu and loyalty-program members) and wants to use it to answer simple questions about his customers: how often they visit, how much they spend and which dishes they like most. This will help him decide whether to expand his customer loyalty program.
